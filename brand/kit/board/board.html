What we heard

The brief

A small builder in Fujisawa building only single-storey homes, at most eight a year, for couples aged 55-65 who have finished raising children. The promise: a morning with no stairs, on one floor as bright as noon. The identity must never sound like welfare or care products, and must not use the category cliches - no house pictogram, no roof, no sunrise, no leaf.

craftedquietexact

Success: distinctive in its category, recognisable at 16 px, works in one colour, and feels like the words above.

Final design

Stepped Joint

Two timbers locked by a stepped seam: the only steps in this company are in the joinery.

  • Drawn from the company's own concept, not the category: MAHIRU removes every step from the floor (principle 1: no level changes indoors) - the single stepped form left in the brand is the stepped joint of Japanese timber joinery.
  • Ownable: checked against a library of 1,400+ real logos; no real mark combines a square block with a stepped joinery seam. Not a house, not a letter, not a sun.
  • Built on a strict grid (160-unit block, 16-unit seam, two steps), single path with a real cut-out seam - it engraves, stamps and cuts as one piece: hanko seal, branding iron, routed sign.
  • Scales: a hand-tuned small-size cut (seam widened to 26 units) keeps the seam legible at 16 px for favicons and SNS avatars.
  • Colour: Mahiru Indigo #1E3A5F - the deep blue of a noon sky and of ai-dyed work cloth; 11.5:1 contrast on white, reproduces in one colour everywhere.
