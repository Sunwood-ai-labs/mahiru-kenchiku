## 🌞 Mission

> A morning without stairs, and one bright floor lit like midday — for the next 30 years of a couple whose children have grown.

"Mahiru" means midday. Life after the children leave is often called the "evening" of life; our clients are not in their evening. They are at noon — the sun at its highest.

## 👥 Who it is for, and what changes

**Who:** Couples aged 55–65 whose children have left home, moving from a 30-year-old two-storey house to a 70–95 m² single-storey home. Service area: Fujisawa, Kamakura, Chigasaki, Hiratsuka and southern Yokohama (within 60 minutes by car). New builds only, at most eight houses a year.

**What:** Not the building itself, but five changes in daily life — mornings with no stairs at all, a bathroom reached without crossing a cold corridor, laundry that starts and finishes on one floor, weekends freed from cleaning unused rooms, and a second bedroom whose name keeps changing (guest room for grandchildren, hobby room, one day a care room).

## 📐 Three design principles

1. **Everything on one floor** — sleeping, washing and laundry all on a single level. No interior steps as standard; sliding doors as standard.
2. **No temperature steps** — insulation at Grade 6 level (target UA ≤ 0.46), with room-to-room differences within 3 °C on a winter morning as the yardstick. No double-height voids.
3. **No fixed room names** — rooms other than the main bedroom are never labelled "children's room" or "guest room". Entrances at least 900 mm clear, with structure in place for future care needs.

## 🏡 Model house — MAHIRU Model 01 "Midday Single-Storey"

A single-storey prototype, 11.0 × 8.5 m with five rooms (living 30 m² / kitchen 25 m² / main bedroom 17.5 m² / bathroom 8.75 m² / flexible room 12.25 m²), a 35° gable roof and nine windows. Built in Pascal Editor and verified by importing into FreeCAD 1.1.4. Data and verification notes: [`model-house/`](model-house/), [`docs/`](docs/).

Honest gaps: the washstand and laundry space are not yet placed, so the wash–dry–put-away flow is still unverified in this model, and insulation performance cannot be checked in the model itself.

## 🎨 Brand

The logo was decided on 1 Nov 2026: **Concept D, "Stepped Joint"** — two timbers locked by a stepped seam. "The only steps in this company are in the joinery." Brand colour: **Mahiru Indigo #1E3A5F**. Final kit: [`brand/kit/`](brand/kit/). The eight concepts (A–H) and their test records remain in [`brand/`](brand/).
